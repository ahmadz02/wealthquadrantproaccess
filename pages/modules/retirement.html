Statutory EPF Compound Accumulation Modeling
$$FV_2 = FV_1 + \left( PMT \times \frac{(1 + R)^n - (1 + G)^n}{R - G} \right)$$

Where:
• $FV_2$ = Total Projected EPF Balance at retirement.
• $FV_1$ = Projected value of current balance $= PV_{\text{epf}} \times (1 + R)^n$.
• $PMT$ = Base contribution $= \text{Current Salary} \times \text{EPF Contribution Rate}$.
• $R$ = EPF Dividend rate, $G$ = Salary Increment rate, and $n$ = years to retirement (25).
